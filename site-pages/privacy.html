Stack Arena privacy

Stack Arena is a free browser game. You can play without an account. Signing in is only needed to put your results on the leaderboard.

What is stored when you sign in

  • Your account from the sign-in provider you chose (GitHub or Google): an account id and the email address and name the provider shares. These are kept by our sign-in service, Supabase.
  • Your leaderboard name, which starts as your username or first name and can be changed on the title screen. It is shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • For each match you finish while signed in: the mode, bot level, number of players, your placement, your gladiator, the match seed and the number of rounds. These are shown publicly as totals on the leaderboard.

What is not done with it

Nothing is sold or shared for advertising. Your email address is never shown to other players. The game keeps your settings and records in your own browser's storage.

Deleting your data

Open an issue at github.com/bameronone/stack-arena/issues asking for your account to be removed, and your account, name and results will be deleted.
